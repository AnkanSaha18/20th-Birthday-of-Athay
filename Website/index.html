From the Editor’s Desk

My dearest Athay,

Every magazine needs a cover star. For this one, there was never a question.

For months I have been quietly collecting you: the photos you laughed at, the ones you pretended not to like, the ones I took when you weren’t looking. Somewhere along the way I realised I wasn’t keeping pictures. I was keeping proof that the best days of my life all have your name on them.

So here it is, your very own issue, now come to life. Twenty chapters for twenty years. It isn’t perfect, but every word in it is true, and every page was made thinking of you.

Happy 20th birthday, my love. Scroll slowly.

Athay,

I don’t always find the right words when I’m standing in front of you, so I wanted to write them down where they can stay. You came into my life and quietly rearranged it. The days are brighter, the future feels closer, and the word “home” has started to sound a lot like your name.

If I could give you one thing for your birthday, it would be the chance to see yourself through my eyes. You would see a girl who lights up a room without trying, who loves with her whole heart, and who makes the most ordinary day feel like a festival.

Twenty looks beautiful on you. I hope this year brings everything you’ve been quietly wishing for: new adventures, big dreams coming true, golden evenings and a hundred reasons to laugh every single day. And through all of it, I hope you always know this: wherever life takes you, you will never have to wonder whether you are loved. You are. Completely. Endlessly. Always.

Never change; let me adapt to you instead. I want to give you my whole world, everything I am. You will dwell forever in the depths of my heart. Stay by my side, as my wife. I love you deeply, and I long to love you infinitely more.

Thank you for every smile, every hug, every “I’m here”. Thank you for your patience with me and for every moment you have chosen to share with me. Thank you for being my best friend, my safe place and my favourite person in the whole world. I know I’m not perfect, but I promise to keep trying, to keep showing up, and to keep loving you a little more each day.

Happy birthday, my love. শুভ জন্মদিন। Here’s to you, to us, and to every page we haven’t written yet.
